Ophel Atlas adds a structured side panel to your AI chat tabs. Instead of scrolling back through hundreds of lines to find one answer or reusing prompts from a scratchpad, you get a live table of contents, folder-and-tag organization, reusable prompt templates, and local-first backups.

All data stays in your browser by default. No account or API key is required.

## Get started in 3 steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Ophel Atlas">
    Install the browser extension for Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or macOS Safari (experimental), or use the userscript on Greasy Fork / ScriptCat. See the [Installation guide](/installation) for direct links and platform differences.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a supported AI site">
    Visit ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek, or any other supported platform. On first launch, accept the local-use disclaimer to start a short 4-step panel tour covering Outline, Chats, Prompts, and Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work from the side panel">
    Use the live outline to jump around long replies, file chats into folders and tags, insert prompt templates, or press <Shortcut combo="ctrl+k" /> to open Global Search.
  </Step>
</Steps>
