Ophel helps you keep your place inside long threads—whether you are reading an earlier paragraph while the AI keeps generating, jumping to the bottom and back, or returning to a thread hours later.

## Reading history and auto-resume

When enabled, Ophel remembers your scroll position for each chat and brings you back to that spot the next time you open it.

- **Availability**: Works on 15 built-in sites (all except Gemini Notebook) and Site Packs that declare `reading-history` (such as DeepSeek Harness).
- **Skip once**: Append `?ophel_skip_restore=1` to a chat URL if you want to open that thread at its default position without restoring the saved offset.

Open **Settings → Features → Reading History**:

| Setting | Options | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Enable Reading History** | On / Off | Off | Saves your scroll position per chat in local storage. |
| **Auto-Resume** | On / Off | On | Automatically scrolls back to your saved position when reopening a chat. |
| **Retention Period** | 1 / 3 / 7 / 30 / 90 days / Forever | 3 days | Automatically cleans up older scroll records after this duration. |

## Scroll lock

Most AI sites keep forcing the page to the bottom as a reply streams. Scroll lock stops that behavior so you can read earlier answers undisturbed:

- **Quick temporary toggle**: Press <Shortcut combo="alt+s" /> or click **Toolbox → Scroll Lock** in the floating quick buttons.
- **Persistent setting**: Turn on **Prevent auto-scroll** under **Settings → Features → Outline** (default **Off**) to keep scroll lock active by default.

<Note>
Scroll lock is unavailable on minimal-mode sites such as Gemini Notebook.
</Note>

## Navigation anchors

Jump across long pages without losing where you were reading:

| Action | Shortcut | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scroll to Top** | <Shortcut combo="alt+t" /> | Saves your current scroll position as an anchor, then jumps to the top of the conversation. |
| **Scroll to Bottom** | <Shortcut combo="alt+b" /> | Saves your current scroll position as an anchor, then jumps to the bottom. |
| **Return to Anchor** | <Shortcut combo="alt+z" /> | Jumps back to the saved anchor. Pressing it again toggles back and forth between the two positions. |

The same three actions are also available in the panel footer and floating quick buttons.
