## Platforms and installation

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which AI platforms does Ophel support?">
    Ophel supports **16 built-in platforms** out of the box (including multi-domain hosts such as Doubao/Dola, Kimi `.com`/`.ai`, and Z.ai) plus **18 community Site Packs** (including self-hosted apps like Open WebUI, LibreChat, and NextChat). See the [full built-in platform table](/introduction#supported-platforms) and [Site Packs Overview](/enhancements/site-extensions).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Should I install the browser extension, Safari build, or userscript?">
    - **Chrome / Edge / Firefox extensions** give you the complete feature set (optional permissions UI, browser-wide <Shortcut combo="alt+o" /> shortcut, background `alarms` for WebDAV auto-backup, Claude browser cookie import, and AI Studio preloads).
    - **Userscript** (Tampermonkey / Violentmonkey / ScriptCat) works in any browser that supports userscripts and includes core features, WebDAV, notifications, and Gemini watermark removal, without browser-wide shortcuts or Claude cookie import.
    - **Safari (macOS)** is experimental and runs as a temporary extension (expires after 24 hours or when Safari quits), without desktop notifications, window auto-focus, or Gemini watermark removal.

    See the full [Platform capability comparison](/installation#platform-capability-comparison).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Ophel free and open source? Do I need an API key?">
    Yes—Ophel Atlas is free and open source under the **GPLv3** license on [GitHub](https://github.com/urzeye/ophel). No account, subscription, or AI API key is needed; Ophel works directly on top of the AI websites you already use.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Features and behavior

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is the Chats tab missing on some sites?">
    Only the **11 built-in platforms** that expose a sidebar chat history list show the **Chats** tab (ChatGPT, Gemini, Gemini Enterprise, AI Studio, Claude, Grok, DeepSeek, Doubao/Dola, Kimi, Qwen Studio, and Yuanbao). Platforms without a sidebar chat list (Gemini Notebook, ChatGLM, ima, Qianwen, Z.ai, and current Site Packs) hide the tab automatically.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Ophel support nested subfolders?">
    Folders are single-level by design, paired with unlimited multi-color **tags** and **pins**. Using one folder per project plus multiple tags lets you drag chats cleanly and filter across categories with `folder:` and `tag:` in Global Search.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do chats mix when I switch accounts, workspaces, or domains?">
    No. Ophel isolates synced chats per account/workspace (such as ChatGPT personal vs. team workspaces and Claude organizations) and isolates local data per domain on multi-domain sites (Doubao vs. Dola, Kimi `.com` vs. `.ai`, Z.ai `chat.z.ai` vs. `zchat.z.ai`) while sharing your Ophel settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="If I delete a chat in Ophel, does it delete the chat on the AI platform too?">
    By default, **yes** — **Delete chat on site when deleting** under **Settings → Features → Chats** is **On**, which also deletes the original chat on supported platforms (irreversible). Turn that switch **Off** if you only want to remove entries from Ophel's local list. Note that cleaning records inside **Missing Chats Review** always removes local metadata only.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I find some settings that used to be directly visible?">
    Low-frequency options in cards like **Outline**, **Export**, **Tab Settings**, **Alerts**, and **Content** are tucked under an **Advanced options** toggle at the bottom of each card to keep everyday settings uncluttered. Two shortcut groups (**Switch Panel Tabs** and **Expand to a Specific Level**) are collapsed the same way. Press <Shortcut combo="ctrl+k" /> to jump directly to any setting by name (legacy names still match).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why are Appearance or Permissions missing from my Settings sidebar?">
    - **Appearance** is hidden when you open the standalone extension Options page directly without a content script attached—open Settings from the side panel inside any supported AI tab instead.
    - **Permissions** is hidden on userscript builds because script managers control host access via `@match` / `@connect` rather than browser extension permission APIs.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Data, sync, and privacy

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where is my data stored, and how do I move to another device?">
    Everything lives locally in your browser's storage (`chrome.storage.local` for extensions, GM storage for userscripts). To move or sync across devices, export a **Full Backup** JSON under **Settings → Data & Permissions → Local Backup**, or set up [WebDAV Sync & Automatic Backups](/data/webdav) with your own WebDAV server.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Ophel send my conversations to any external server?">
    Never. Your chat messages stay on the AI platforms you visit. See [Privacy](/data/privacy) for the complete list of optional network requests Ophel can make (WebDAV to your own server, signed Site Pack index checks, and the voluntary uninstall survey).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I reset everything and start fresh?">
    Go to **Settings → Data & Permissions → Local Backup** and click **Clear All Data** at the bottom. This clears local Ophel data only and never deletes your chats on AI websites.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
