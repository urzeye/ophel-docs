Long AI sessions get hard to read and harder to find later. Most chat sites give you one flat, scrolling column with little or no way to jump between sections, group threads by topic, or reuse prompt workflows across tools.

Ophel Atlas runs inside your AI chat pages and adds the same structured workspace across platforms: a live outline, folder-and-tag organization, reusable prompts, flexible exports, and reading controls.

## Core capabilities

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Smart Outline" icon="/images/icons/outline.svg" href="/features/outline">
    Builds a live heading and question tree as replies stream in, with level folding, keyword filtering, bookmarks, word counts, and section copy.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Chat Management & Export" icon="/images/icons/folder.svg" href="/features/conversations">
    Organize synced chats into folders and tags, pin key threads, run batch actions, and export full or selected turns to Markdown, ZIP (with images), HTML, JSON, TXT, or shareable images.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Prompt Library, Queues & Chains" icon="/images/icons/prompt.svg" href="/features/prompts">
    Store templates with variables (`{{var}}`, defaults, dropdown options), quote text directly from replies, queue prompts to send in order, and chain multi-step instructions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Global Search" icon="/images/icons/search.svg" href="/features/global-search">
    Press <Shortcut combo="ctrl+k" /> to search your current outline, synced chats across sites, prompts, and settings with `type:`, `folder:`, `tag:`, `is:`, `level:`, and `date:` filters.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reading & Content Enhancements" icon="/images/icons/page-content.svg" href="/enhancements/content">
    Render Markdown in your own questions, double-click formulas to copy LaTeX or MathML, copy tables as Markdown, render Mermaid diagrams, lock auto-scroll, and resume reading positions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Appearance, Layout & Site Controls" icon="/images/icons/appearance.svg" href="/customization/appearance">

## Privacy and local-first design

- **Stored on your device**: Settings, folders, tags, prompts, bookmarks, and reading history live in your browser's local storage (or your userscript manager's local storage).
- **No Ophel account or tracking**: Ophel has no login, no ads, and no in-extension analytics.
- **Minimal optional network access**: Ophel only talks to the network when you configure your own WebDAV server, when Site Packs checks its signed read-only update index (every 24 hours when auto-update is enabled), or if you choose to submit the optional uninstall survey. See [Privacy](/data/privacy) and [Permissions](/data/permissions).
